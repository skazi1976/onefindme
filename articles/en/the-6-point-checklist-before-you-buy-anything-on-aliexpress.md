# The 6-point checklist before you buy anything on AliExpress

*Originally published at [rentry.co](https://rentry.co/okzu47xa) · OneFindMe article archive · [onefindme.com](https://onefindme.com/)*

Most bad AliExpress purchases are not scams. They are avoidable mistakes made in the thirty
seconds before clicking "buy". Run this list every time.

## 1. Did you find the product, or a keyword's idea of it?

If you searched an English keyword you were not sure of, you may be looking at the wrong thing
entirely (a "facial roller" and a "gua sha" are different products). When you can only describe
what you want, use a tool that understands descriptions: [OneFindMe](https://onefindme.com/en/)
is a free search engine that takes your phrase in any of 12 languages and returns the matching
listings, ranked by price and shipping. Confirm the *product* before you compare *prices*.

## 2. Orders and reviews, not stars

A 4.8 rating from 12 buyers means nothing. Look for hundreds of orders and read the 3-star
reviews — they say what the photos leave out.

## 3. Photos from buyers, not the seller

Seller photos are renders. Buyer photos are reality. If there are none, that is information too.

## 4. The size chart, measured in centimetres

Never trust S/M/L. Compare the chart's centimetres to a garment you already own.

## 5. Shipping to *your* country, with the date

Set your destination before comparing. The estimated delivery date and any customs note are
part of the price.

## 6. The seller's store, not just the listing

Click through to the store. Years of history and thousands of followers beat a fresh account
with a slightly lower price.

Six checks, thirty seconds, and the "cheap" mistakes stop happening.
