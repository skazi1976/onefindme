# How to Find the Right Product on AliExpress When You Don't Know Its Exact Name

*Originally published at [telegra.ph](https://telegra.ph/How-to-Find-the-Right-Product-on-AliExpress-When-You-Dont-Know-Its-Exact-Name-09-20) · OneFindMe article archive · [onefindme.com](https://onefindme.com/)*

AliExpress lists hundreds of millions of products, but its search only rewards you when you already know the exact English keyword. If you can only describe what you want, the results turn to noise. Here are five practical ways to find the right product anyway.

### 1. Describe what you want instead of guessing the keyword

Is it a "gua sha", a "facial roller", or a "massage stone"? When you don't know the precise term, plain keyword search fails. A free search tool like [OneFindMe](https://onefindme.com/en/) lets you type what you want in everyday language — in any of 12 languages — turns it into the right AliExpress query, and ranks the best matches by price and shipping. It's the fastest fix when the product is easy to picture but hard to name.

### 2. Use image search when you have a photo

Saw the item on Instagram or a friend's phone? Upload the photo to AliExpress' built-in image search (the little camera icon in the search bar). It matches by shape and pattern, so you skip the wording problem entirely. Crop the photo to just the product first for cleaner results.

### 3. Sort by orders and rating, not only price

The cheapest listing is rarely the best. Sort by "Orders" to surface the products real buyers actually purchase, then read the 3-star reviews — they are the most honest about sizing, quality, and what the photos leave out.

### 4. Open the seller's store, not just the listing

Click the store name under a good product to see everything that seller offers. A store with thousands of followers and years of history is far safer than a brand-new one, even if the single listing looks identical.

### 5. Check shipping to your country before you fall in love

Set your destination country at the top of the page so prices and delivery times reflect reality. A great deal with 45-day shipping or a surprise customs fee is not a great deal.

Put together, these turn AliExpress from a wall of noise into something you can actually search. And when you truly can't find the words, let a tool do the translating — that's exactly what [OneFindMe](https://onefindme.com/en/) was built for.
