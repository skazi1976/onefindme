Can you search AliExpress by voice? In the AliExpress app, a little — the microphone mostly understands English and turns your words into the same literal keyword search that makes typed queries fail. In your own language, describing what you want out loud, and getting ranked results? That, as far as I can find, exists in exactly one place.

### How voice search on AliExpress works today

The AliExpress app's mic sends your speech through the same keyword matcher as the search bar. Say "a small stone for face massage" and you get whatever listings contain those words — often nothing useful, because sellers call it a "gua sha". In Hebrew, Arabic, Turkish or Polish, recognition is weaker and the translation is word-for-word.

### When voice beats typing

- **You can picture it but can't name it.** "The thing that holds a baby's pacifier" is a fine voice query.

- **You're on your phone with one hand.** Speaking a description is faster than typing it.

- **English isn't your first language.** Speak in yours; the translation to the marketplace's terms is the engine's job.

### Tips for good voice results

- Say the **product first**, then the details: "running shoes, wide, under forty dollars".

- Set your **country** so shipping and price are real.

- If recognition misses a word, tap the text box and fix it — the query is editable before it runs.
