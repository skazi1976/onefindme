# How to Find Any Product on AliExpress — Even When You Don't Know Its Name

*Originally published at [best-deals-finder.blogspot.com](https://best-deals-finder.blogspot.com/2026/09/how-to-find-any-product-on-aliexpress.html) · OneFindMe article archive · [onefindme.com](https://onefindme.com/)*

AliExpress lists hundreds of millions of products, but its search only works when you already know the exact English keyword. If you can only *describe* what you want, the results turn to noise. Here is how to find it anyway.

### 1. Describe it instead of guessing the keyword
Is it a "gua sha", a "facial roller", or a "massage stone"? When you don't know the term, plain search fails. A free tool like **[OneFindMe](https://onefindme.com/en/)** lets you type what you want in everyday language — in any of 12 languages — turns it into the right query, and ranks the best matches by price and shipping.

### 2. Use image search
Saw it on Instagram? Upload the photo to AliExpress' camera-icon search — it matches by shape, so you skip the wording problem entirely.

### 3. Sort by orders, not price
The cheapest listing is rarely the best. Sort by Orders to see what real buyers purchase, and read the 3-star reviews — they're the most honest.

### 4. Check shipping to your country first
A great deal with 45-day shipping isn't a great deal. Set your country so prices and delivery times are real before you commit.
