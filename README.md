# OneFindMe

**Free AI-powered product search for AliExpress, in 12 languages.**

Website: **https://onefindme.com**

## What it is

AliExpress lists hundreds of millions of products, but its search only works if you already know the exact English keyword. OneFindMe removes that barrier: you describe what you want in your own language — "a small stone for face massage", "wide running shoes", "ceramic pour-over coffee dripper" — and it returns the best-matching AliExpress products, ranked by price and shipping to your country.

- **Describe, don't guess.** Plain-language queries in any supported language.
- **Intent, not word-for-word translation.** Your phrase is resolved to the product concept first, then turned into the query the marketplace actually responds to.
- **Ranked by what matters.** Real order volume and reviews, then price and shipping — not just the cheapest listing.
- **Free, no account.** Nothing to install, nothing to sign up for.

## How the search works

1. **Understand the intent.** The query is mapped to a canonical product concept. A curated keyword map handles the high-traffic terms exactly; an AI model covers the long tail.
2. **Shape the query.** Marketplace search rewards short, noun-led queries, so the concept is trimmed to the product noun plus one or two qualifiers before any call is made.
3. **Rank on behaviour.** Candidates are ordered by order volume and review presence, then filtered by price and shipping to the shopper's country.
4. **Cache the resolution, not the stock.** The mapping from a phrase to a good query is stable and is cached; product availability is fetched fresh.

## Tools on the site

Alongside search, onefindme.com hosts free shopping tools — currency converter, size charts and customs/import-duty guides — plus product guides and roundups in every supported language.
